### Published conference paper

**[Privacy by Design: A Review of Safeguarding Strategies for LLMs in Critical AI Applications](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-22299-2_15)**

- **Type:** Conference paper (I2COMSAPP 2025), second author
- **Published in:** *Artificial Intelligence and its Practical Applications*, Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems, vol. 1917, Springer, Cham, pp. 226–237
- **Published online:** 29 May 2026
- **Co-authors:** Fouzi Harrag, Maria Dib, Mohamed Deriche, Yahya Mohamed Elhadj
- **Relevance:** A survey of techniques for safeguarding LLMs in high-stakes domains such as healthcare, covering privacy-preserving training, cryptographic safeguards, reasoning-augmented defenses, and adversarially robust architectures, with a threat taxonomy.
- **My contributions:** research idea, literature review, scientific writing, research classification, and architectural design.
